# Activity 2: Personal Branding

## Short Description
This activity introduced personal branding and its importance in creating a unique identity that influences how others perceive an individual.

## What Have I Learned?
I learned that personal branding is about presenting my identity, strengths, values, and skills in a way that makes me recognizable. It also helped me understand the importance of consistency in how I communicate and present myself online and offline. One challenge is identifying the qualities that best represent me, which requires self-reflection and awareness of my strengths. In the future, I can apply what I learned by building a professional online presence and maintaining a positive personal image.
